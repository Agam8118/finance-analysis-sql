\# Finance Analytics — Analysis Results



\## 1. Project Overview



This project uses MySQL 8.0 to analyse fictional personal finance transactions, monthly spending patterns, and budget performance.



The analysis covers January to June 2026 across eight expense categories.



\## 2. Budget Performance



The database contains 48 monthly category-budget records.



Key findings:



\- \*\*Housing:\*\* Stayed within its monthly budget of ₹12,000 in all six months.

\- \*\*Shopping:\*\* Exceeded its budget in February, April, and May. The largest overspend was ₹1,700 in May.

\- \*\*Entertainment:\*\* Exceeded its budget in February, March, and June. The largest overspend was ₹1,000 in March.

\- \*\*Education:\*\* Exceeded its budget in March and May.

\- \*\*Food:\*\* Exceeded its budget in May by ₹400.

\- \*\*Healthcare:\*\* Exceeded its budget in February and May.

\- \*\*Transport:\*\* Exceeded its budget in March and May.

\- \*\*Utilities:\*\* Exceeded its budget in February and June.

\## 4. Key Insights



1\. Total spending over the six-month period was ₹147,000 against a combined budget of ₹151,800.

2\. Overall spending was ₹4,800 below the combined budget, although some categories exceeded their individual limits.

3\. May recorded the highest monthly spending at ₹28,100.

4\. March and May were the only months when total spending exceeded the combined monthly category budgets.

5\. Shopping showed the largest individual category overspend, reaching ₹1,700 in May.

6\. Housing was the most consistent category, with actual spending matching its budget every month.

\## 6. Limitations



The dataset is fictional and covers only six months. These results demonstrate SQL analysis techniques and should not be interpreted as conclusions about real household finances.
